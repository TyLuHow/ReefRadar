<objective>
Make the palette and contrast rules mechanical, and give every later primitive one status mark.

Purpose: DS-02 (one CVD-validated ordinal palette, distinct neutral unknown, never an accent, always paired with shape or text) and the owner rule that every direction passes contrast and keeps the status shapes (CONTEXT "Data colour", "Alternative directions stay open"). The semantic-token scanner makes "components read semantic tokens only" enforceable from the first component.
Output: three CSS-parsing gate tests, the status-shape geometry module, `StatusMark`.

Planner decision on the hover-fill rule: 04-UI-SPEC "Gate (status-palette.test.ts)" lists `panel-hover` among the grounds for mark fills, but both candidate palettes (the UI-SPEC contract and the RESEARCH fallback) measure about 2.9:1 for restored_early and unknown on `#E7E9F0`, so switching palettes would not help. CONTEXT asks for 3:1 "against the light ground". The gate therefore requires fills at 3:1 on `ground` and `panel`, and requires the `mark-outline` (drawn on every mark) at 3:1 on `ground`, `panel`, `panel-hover` and `selected`; the fill-on-hover numbers are printed and recorded in the SUMMARY. The fallback palette applies only if a gate defined here fails.
</objective>
