    - An aborted signal rejects promptly with an AbortError and stops polling; exceeding maxWaitMs (default 180000) rejects with a timeout AnalysisError.
    - /dashboard/analyze shows the current stage text in AnalysisProgress (no numeric percentage) and, on failure, the message, suggestion and request id.
  </behavior>
